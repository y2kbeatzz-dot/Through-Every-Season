# Through Every Season

A compassionate space for self-harm and suicide awareness, coping, connection, and music. Created & designed by Crystal.

[Visit the website](https://y2kbeatzz-dot.github.io/Through-Every-Season/)

## What's here

- Four seasonal themes and Halloween, Christmas, Valentine’s Day, and New Year themes.
- Gentle motion off by default, with reduced-motion support.
- Coping ideas, guidance for supporting a friend, and clearly labeled crisis support.
- Optional YouTube music: One More Light, Rise Up, and Hold On. Nothing autoplays.
- Search metadata, canonical URL, structured data, and sitemap.xml.
- GoatCounter integration ready for setup; no fabricated view numbers.



This educational website is not a live counseling service. Resources link to the NHS, 988 Lifeline, and SAMHSA. Theme and motion preferences are browser-local. YouTube loads after choosing a song. Once configured, GoatCounter collects analytics under its own privacy policy. No account, personal story, or health details are collected by this website.
